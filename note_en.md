# VCB Bank Account Opening Process for New Employees

> **Legend:**
> - **STK** — bank account number (số tài khoản)
> - **NLĐ** — employee (người lao động)
> - **C&B** — C&B / HR department
> - **V / G** — Problem / Solution
> - **action** — the follow-up needed to finish opening the account, assigned by VCB. Example: remind/call the employee to re-sign the paperwork, complete eKYC, ...

---

## 1. Q&A

**Q:** Why doesn't VCB handle account opening directly at the counter and require filling out a form?

**A:** Because the form + opening the account via the Digibank app supports **many people at the same time**, while handling at the VCB counter takes only **one person at a time**.
The form still guarantees the account is opened within **1–2 days** (exceptions: must come back to re-sign due to signature mismatch, incomplete signature on the paperwork, or insufficient information).

---

## 2. Problems & Solutions

### V1 — Not all employees can get through the Vietcombank counter

**Why an employee is missing an account number (only 2 cases):**
1. Has not been to the counter
2. Assumes they already have an account number, but the account has actually been closed

**G1:** Make **everyone go to the VCB counter** and obtain confirmation from C&B staff before moving on to the onboarding step.

### V2 — No internet, no devices

**G2:** VCB staff provide Wi-Fi and lend devices when needed.

### V3 — Employee arrives late (starts the job right after the interview)

**G3:** Adjust VCB's support time slots — when it gets crowded, employees can be supported at **midday** (after lunch).

### V4 — Account number not issued on time

**G4:** Change the working standard:

- On the start date, once the list of new employees is available, VCB sends it to C&B.
- Within the **next 3 days**, VCB is responsible for sending account number info + action for the cases:
  - not supported via the form, **and**
  - unable to reach the person (with evidence).
- **C&B** is responsible for reminding the assistant and tracking the status of people still missing an account number.

## 4. Process Timeline

| When | Event |
|------|-------|
| **Onboard Day** (start date) | C&B sends the list of new employees to VCB |
| **Onboard Day** | 100% of employees pass through the VCB counter (or fill out the form + open the account via Digibank); VCB staff provide Wi-Fi, lend devices if needed |
| **Onboard Day** | C&B staff confirms each person has passed through the counter → then moves on to the onboarding step |
| **Onboard Day – Onboard+3** | VCB processes the forms and opens the accounts (normally 1–2 days) |
| **Before Onboard+3** | VCB sends account number + action for cases not supported via the form / unable to reach the person (with evidence) |
| **Daily from Onboard** | C&B reminds the assistant and tracks the status of people still missing an account number until 100% |

---

## 5. Responsibilities

| Task | Owner | Deadline |
|------|-------|----------|
| Send the list of new employees to VCB | C&B | Onboard Day |
| Organize employees to the counter / support form + Digibank | VCB + C&B | Onboard Day |
| Provide Wi-Fi, lend devices | VCB staff | As needed |
| Confirm each person has passed through the counter (a condition for starting the job) | C&B staff | Onboard Day |
| Send account number + action for failure cases (with evidence) | VCB | Within 3 days of receiving the list |
| Remind the assistant about people still missing | C&B | Daily |
| Track status until 100% have an account number | C&B | Daily |

---

## 6. C&B Checklist

- [ ] Send the list of new employees to VCB
- [ ] Ensure 100% of employees pass through the VCB counter or have filled out the form
- [ ] Confirm each person (signed/acknowledged) before moving on to onboarding
- [ ] Reconcile the list with account numbers after 3 days
- [ ] Receive account number + action for failure cases (VCB evidence required)
- [ ] Remind the assistant about people still missing
- [ ] Track & update status until everyone has an account number
